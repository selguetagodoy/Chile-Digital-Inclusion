# Key findings

- CASEN national weighted population: 20,131,682.
- Any Internet access: 97.49%; fixed: 71.83%; mobile-only: 24.62%; no Internet: 2.51%.
- Digital-connectivity deprivation: 12.96%.
- Austral macrozone has the highest deprivation in the master: 19.54%; the South has the highest double poverty: 6.48%.
- Age is strongly associated with digital deprivation: 9.43% at ages 30–44 versus 28.83% at 75+.
- Highest regional digital deprivation: Ñuble 24.95%, Los Lagos 22.53%, Atacama 21.90%, La Araucanía 21.57%.
- SUBTEL 2025 reports 96.6% household paid Internet access, but 83.2% among households composed only of older people.
- The repository keeps household, weighted-person and network-performance universes separate.
