# Methodology

Chile Digital Inclusion is a public research repository for measuring digital exclusion beyond simple network coverage.

The project distinguishes access, type of connection, device availability, functional use, social inequality, territorial differences and observed network quality.

## Statistical units

The repository does not merge percentages from different universes as if they were equivalent.

- Censo 2024 and the public Atlas household layer use households and territory as their principal units.
- CASEN 2024 uses weighted persons (`N_ponderado`) for the public extracts in this repository.
- SUBTEL survey access results refer to households. Its use section refers to people aged 16 or older.
- Ookla observations describe network performance, not household or person access.

This distinction explains why a Censo/Atlas household disconnection percentage may differ from the CASEN weighted-person estimate without either result being intrinsically contradictory.

## Evidence layers

### Censo 2024 / public Atlas layer

Used for national and regional household disconnection, technology and territorial indicators.

### CASEN Digital Master 2024

Used for national and macrozone estimates plus regional and commune connectivity rankings. The public release retains weighted population and aggregate percentages. Small-area survey estimates should be interpreted with caution.

### SUBTEL Access and Use of Internet surveys

The official SUBTEL catalogue is tracked separately. The current Duodécima Encuesta was published under 2026 with fieldwork conducted from June to September 2025. It used 5,000 in-person interviews across urban and rural areas of Chile's 16 regions.

### Network quality

Ookla Q4 2025 metrics are maintained as a separate performance layer. They are not treated as measures of household access.

## Publication rule

Only public or aggregated observations are released. Missing values remain missing. The repository does not publish person-level microdata, direct identifiers, the complete Digital Vulnerability Index, proprietary weights, private datasets or commercial models.

Internet access is not treated as equivalent to digital inclusion. A household or person can report connectivity while remaining functionally constrained by mobile-only access, lack of a suitable device, affordability, limited skills or poor service quality.
